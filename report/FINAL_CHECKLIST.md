## Errors found and fixed during the build (for transparency)
- PDF parsing: ratio columns read as values, a CJK glyph on a segment "Total" row, section anchors matching body text.
- Price data: UTC date shift; vendor price error in 1306.T on 30-31 Mar 2026 (distorted monthly betas).
- Valuation: the first exit multiple (9.0x, then 8.5x) implied perpetual growth above the risk-free rate; replaced with a
  consistency-checked multiple and a value-driver terminal value. This moved the rating from BUY (+16%) to HOLD (+11%).
- Text: Europe FY2026 local-currency growth corrected from -1.6% to -1.5%; FY2022 product-mix values and a gross-margin
  claim corrected; an unverifiable Bosch figure replaced with a verified one.
