01 · Investment thesis

Three reasons to hold, not chase

Makita owns one of the strongest battery-tool platforms in the world and a fortress balance sheet. The price already reflects the recovery.

I

The margin recovery is complete; the next leg needs cost discipline

Gross margin is back to , close to FY2018's . The gap to the operating-margin target is overhead: SG&A rose from to of sales while the sales force grows.

II

Growth is currency, not volume

Europe is of sales and shrank in local currency in FY2026. 1Q FY2027 revenue rose in yen but was flat in local currency, while Milwaukee grew .

III

The balance sheet is the lever

Net cash of caps ROE at against an target. The stock trades on EV/EBITDA, below its own median: limited downside, buybacks are the trigger.

⇄

What would make me wrong

Turn to Buy

Operating margin heads to 15% without giving up growth; a sustained ~10% weaker yen; large buybacks lifting ROE toward 11%.

Turn to Sell

A second inventory cycle: inventory days above 300, European local-currency sales falling more than 3%, gross margin back to 34-35%.

05 · Method

Every number is traceable

Raw filings are saved untouched, parsed by code, checked, and fed into an Excel model with live formulas. Hover a dotted number on this page to see its source.

  1. 01

    Collect

    Makita IFRS releases and audited statements, MoF JGB yields, Damodaran, SEC, yfinance. Every file logged with a checksum.

  2. 02

    Parse and check

    PDF statements parsed to tidy tables; integrity checks; restatements flagged.

  3. 03

    Model

    Three statements, regional revenue x FX, DCF, comps and sensitivities in Excel, recalculated headlessly; all checks pass in all scenarios.

  4. 04

    Publish

    The PDF report, this site and the model are generated from the same outputs.
